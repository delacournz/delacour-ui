Add `Questionnaire` — a guided form, one question at a time. Six question kinds (single and multiple choice with a free-text "Other", text, rating, a numbered scale and yes/no), conditional questions that drop their stale answers, Back that follows the questions actually visited, deliberate skips, a dimmed-not-disabled Next that explains a blocked answer, async submit, controlled answers and navigation, and bar, stepper or count progress. The flow's rules are exported as pure functions, so a server can re-validate a submission.
