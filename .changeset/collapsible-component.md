Add `Collapsible` — one section of content shown and hidden by its own trigger. Controlled (`isOpen` / `onOpenChange`) or uncontrolled (`defaultOpen`), with `Collapsible.Trigger`, `.Title`, `.Description`, `.Indicator` and `.Content`. The panel's height is measured and sprung, so whatever sits below follows it; the panel mounts on first open and stays mounted, and a closed panel leaves the accessibility tree. Shares `Accordion`'s variants, sizes and motion, and `bunx delacour add collapsible` copies it without pulling in an accordion.
