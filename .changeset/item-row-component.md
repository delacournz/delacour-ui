Add `Item`, a row of media, text and actions for lists and settings. It renders as a `Pressable` when given `onPress` and as a plain view otherwise, has `default`, `outline` and `muted` surfaces, `sm`/`md`/`lg` sizes, a `vertical` orientation for cards, `isDisabled` and `isSelected` states, and `Item.Media` (bare icon, icon tile or clipped image), `Item.Content`, `Item.Title`, `Item.Description`, `Item.Actions`, `Item.Header`, `Item.Footer`, `Item.Group` and `Item.Separator`. Dropped into a `ListGroup` it takes the group's size, draws no surface of its own and lines up with the group's dividers. The CLI can now `add item`.
