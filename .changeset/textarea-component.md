Add `Textarea`, a multiline text field sized in rows. It renders `Input`'s box — variants, sizes, focus, invalid, disabled and the `Field` cascade — and adds a height derived from `rows` and the size's line height, `autoGrow` up to `maxRows`, and a `showCount` character count against `maxLength` that turns destructive at the limit. `delacour add textarea` copies it, with `input` and `text` as registry dependencies.
