Sixteen more components read their spoken and visible strings from the `DelacourProvider` `labels` catalog and take a `labels` prop of their own: Drawer, Fab (on `Fab.Group`), Feedback, FlipCard, Flow, ImageViewer, Leaderboard, Map, MarkdownEditor, Menu, Message, MessageScroller, NumberInput, OtpInput, PageHeader and Planner. Every default is today's English, so nothing changes until an app passes labels. The per-string props these components already had (`accessibilityLabel`, `accessibilityHint`, `emptyText`, `faceLabels`, `sentMessage`, `failedMessage`, `previousLabel`, `nextLabel`, a part's `label` and children) still win over `labels`. The pure resolvers take the labels as a defaulted trailing argument, so existing calls keep their English. The exported English constants (`FLIP_CARD_ACTION_LABEL`, `FLIP_CARD_DEFAULT_FACE_LABELS`, `FLOW_CONTROL_LABELS`, `MAP_ACCESSIBILITY_LABEL`, `MARKDOWN_ACTION_LABELS`, `MENU_TRIGGER_HINT`, `OTP_DEFAULT_LABEL`, `FEEDBACK_SENT_MESSAGE`, `FEEDBACK_FAILED_MESSAGE`, `DEFAULT_JUMP_LABEL`) are now aliases of the catalog's defaults; `NumberInput`'s internal `NUMBER_INPUT_LIMIT_LABELS` is gone. ContextMenu, which renders `Menu.Content`, speaks Menu's `close` label.
