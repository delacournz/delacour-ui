Add `ScrollCanvas`, an image in a still frame that moves as the page scrolls — `parallax`, `zoom`, `reveal`, or a `sequence` of frames scrubbed by the scroll. It follows a `Screen` with no wiring or a `useScrollSource` source at any depth, takes a `progress` shared value to drive it directly, and draws each effect's resting state under Reduce Motion.
