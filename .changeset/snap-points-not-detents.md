Detents are snap points everywhere, matching the `snapPoints` prop. `DetentSpec` is `SnapPointSpec`, `onDetentHaptic` is `onSnapPointHaptic`, `useBottomSheetAnimated()`'s `detents` is `snapPoints`, the internal `detentCount` is `snapPointCount`, and the core helpers follow (`normalizeSnapPoints`, `parseSnapPoint`, `dynamicSnapPoint`, `crossedSnapPoint`, `snapPointUnder`). The handle's accessibility value reads "Snap point 1 of 2". The docs page moves to `/docs/bottom-sheet/snap-points`.
