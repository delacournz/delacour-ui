Add `ContextMenu` — the actions that belong to a piece of content, reached by holding it. A hold opens the menu at the finger or against the whole trigger; a short press runs the trigger's `onPress`, and never both. `ContextMenu.Preview` lifts a copy of the held content over the scrim, with the panel beside it. The rows and the panel are Menu's own, and `Menu.Content` gains a `backdrop` prop for drawing over its scrim.
